# Align To Melody

## Status

Implemented in Yass Reincarnated, inherited from Yass Reloaded. Covers full,
timing-only, and pitch-only alignment.

## Purpose

Let authors adjust selected notes to the sung melody after manual editing,
tapping, or note generation.

## Functional Requirements

- **Align to Melody** (`M`) adjusts pitch, start, and duration.
  **Align Timing** (`Ctrl+M`) preserves pitch; **Align Pitch** (`Shift+M`)
  preserves start and duration.
- Alignment requires selected notes and available detected pitch. It does not
  run while editing lyric text or the song header.
- Alignment follows the visible pitch-data transpose and does not change
  persistent settings.
- Manual alignment keeps detected octave outliers within the C3–G5 vocal range
  while preserving pitch class. Notes generated by the wizard or tapping retain
  their detected octave, subject to the continuity rule: a detected pitch above
  C5 is lowered one octave when the previous note is at least an octave lower.
  That continuity rule also applies to manual alignment.
- Timing follows the strong sung body, trimming weak onset/trailing tails,
  respecting internal silence, and avoiding collisions between selected notes.
  A vocal tail from the previous page must not become the next page's first note.
- Manual pitch alignment uses the melody within the note's original time range;
  a vocal lead-in before that note must not replace its pitch.
- Generated notes may extend into unused vocal lead-ins after page breaks.
- A musical key in `#COMMENT:key=...` breaks an exact tie between pitches; it
  does not force all detected notes into that key.

## Acceptance Scenarios

- Select notes with usable pitch data and use Align Timing. Their start and
  duration follow the vocals while their pitches remain unchanged.
- Use Align Pitch on the same notes. Pitches change while timing stays unchanged.
- Align a note spanning a strong vocal body and a weak trailing tail. The note
  ends with the sung body rather than extending through the tail.
- Manually align the first note on a page when an earlier vocal lead-in has a
  different pitch. The note uses the pitch inside its original range.
- Generate notes from tapping or the wizard with an unused vocal lead-in after
  a page break. Alignment can include that lead-in.

## Scope And Constraints

Alignment adjusts existing or generated notes; it does not supply new lyrics.
Transcript-based timing has its own
[alignment requirements](transcription-alignment.md).
